# Monetization Playbook - ecotide

This file is a commercial planning artifact, not a guarantee of revenue. Use it to make the repository legible as a sellable product, pilot, workshop, license, or proof-of-value package.

## Commercial Lane

- **Lane:** B2C/B2B education simulation
- **Primary buyer:** Mobile product reviewers, educators, workshop creators, and SwiftUI teams.
- **Revenue wedge:** Native simulation app showing motion telemetry, scenario replay, and calm mobile craft.

## Offer Ladder

1. **Paid educational app prototype** - pricing hypothesis: $2-$5 app
2. **Workshop demo license** - pricing hypothesis: $500-$2k workshop license
3. **Native UX case-study package** - pricing hypothesis: $2k-$8k UX prototype engagement

## Acquisition Channels

- SwiftUI demo reels
- Education creator outreach
- Climate/simulation workshop materials

## Proof Gates Before Selling

- CLI smoke passes
- Motion fallback is explicit
- Review pack is generated

## Risk Boundaries

- Telemetry privacy
- Avoid scientific accuracy overclaims
- Motion data opt-in

## 30/60/90 Day GTM

- **30 days:** Create 3 classroom scenarios
- **60 days:** Record simulator walkthrough
- **90 days:** Offer workshop kit

## Metrics That Matter

- Scenario completions
- Workshop adoption
- App retention

## Positioning Note

Lead with the specific operational pain, then show the runnable proof, then offer a narrow pilot. Do not pitch the repository as a vague app; pitch it as a concrete workflow with evidence, risk boundaries, and a next buying step.
